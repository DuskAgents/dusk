    Mechanism
    
      
    Four moments,
    from light to dark.
    
      
    DUSK sits between your agent and every payment it makes. It never holds funds. It decides, then it seals.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    01
    Golden hour
    Sign the grant.
    You set a budget, a per-call cap, a daily cap and an expiry, then sign them with your wallet. A signature, not a transaction: no gas, nothing on-chain.
    
          
    02
    Sunset
    Hand over the key.
    Your browser creates the agent key and a sealing key. DUSK keeps only a hash of the key, and a copy of the sealing key locked to your wallet.
    
          
    03
    Civil dusk
    Spend within bounds.
    Before each payment the agent asks DUSK. Over a cap, past expiry, paused or revoked: refused. Inside the bounds: approved with a receipt.
    
          
    04
    Nautical dusk
    Read in the dark.
    Each approved payment is sealed with the agent's key and chained to the previous one by hash. Only your wallet unlocks the ledger.

    For agents
    
        
    Ask first,
    then pay.
    
        
    Open source on GitHub. Your agent holds its own wallet. Before every payment it asks DUSK, and pays the seller only when the answer is ok: true.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    i
    The agent key
    Created in the vault, shown once. Three parts joined by dots: grantId.token.sealingKey. Send only grantId.token as the bearer. The sealing key never leaves your agent.
    
        
    ii
    Three endpoints
    
          
    GET/api/agent
    Limits, spent, remaining, remaining today, status.
    
          
    POST/api/check
    Same body without sealed. A dry run: same answer as spend, nothing is spent or recorded.
    
          
    POST/api/spend
    Body { amount, sealed, tag }. Amount is a USDC string, up to 6 decimals. Sealed is AES-GCM ciphertext, base64. Tag is the seller's domain keyed with the sealing key, checked against the grant's private allowlist.
    
          
    Answers: 200 approved · 402 over a limit · 403 paused, revoked, expired or seller not allowed · 401 bad key · 429 over 120 calls a minute.
    
        
    iii
    Drop-in client
    
          
    Node 18+, Deno, Bun or any browser. No dependencies.
    
          
    
        
    iv
    Any agent.
    Plug DUSK into Claude, Cursor or any MCP agent with the DUSK MCP server. Your agent gets three tools: dusk_limits, dusk_check and dusk_spend. Prefer Python? Same calls, one file. Both are on GitHub.

    Questions
    
        
    Asked after dark.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Does DUSK hold my money?
    No. Your agent pays from its own wallet. DUSK approves or refuses each payment before it happens, then seals the record. Nothing to deposit, nothing to withdraw.
    
        
    Is anything written on-chain?
    No. A grant is a wallet signature over its terms. It costs no gas, and anyone can verify it in the Proof section.
    
        
    Who can read the ledger?
    Only the wallet that signed the grant. Each entry is sealed with the grant's key, and that key is stored locked to a key derived from your wallet signature in your browser. DUSK stores ciphertext.
    
        
    What does DUSK see?
    The amount of each payment, because limits are enforced on it, and its time. Seller, request and note are sealed by your agent before they reach DUSK.
    
        
    What if a key leaks?
    Pause or revoke the grant in your vault. It applies to the next call. Revoked is final: sign a new grant and hand over the new key.
    
        
    Can someone tamper with the ledger?
    Each entry carries the hash of the one before, starting from a value tied to your signature. Your vault checks the whole chain every time it opens a ledger.
    
        
    Which wallets work?
    Any browser wallet that supports personal_sign: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet and others. Your vault key comes from a signature, so use the same wallet each time.
    
        
    What does $DUSK unlock?
    Hold the holder amount in the wallet you sign with: up to fifty active grants instead of three, private allowlists and on-chain anchors. DUSK reads your balance on Robinhood Chain when you open your vault and when you create a grant.
    
        
    How can DUSK enforce an allowlist it cannot read?
    Your browser turns each seller domain into a tag keyed with the grant's sealing key, which DUSK never has. Your agent computes the same tag before each payment. DUSK only compares tags, so it can refuse a seller without knowing who it is. The list itself is committed in the grant you sign.
    
        
    What is an anchor?
    A zero-value transaction from your wallet to itself on Robinhood Chain, carrying your grant id, a ledger position and its hash. Anyone can read it on the explorer. If a single past entry were changed, the hash chain would no longer match the anchor.
    
        
    What is $DUSK?
    The token of DUSK on Robinhood Chain. Its contract address is pinned at the top of this page. Always copy it from here or from the official X account.
